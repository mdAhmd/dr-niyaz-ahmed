How the consultation works

First we listen.
Then we explain.

A good dermatology consultation is not just a prescription. It is a process of listening, examining, explaining and planning.

01

We see & listen

We take the time to understand your symptoms, history, concerns and what you have already tried.

02

At least 30 minutes

The consultation is designed to give you at least 30 minutes for discussion and clinical assessment, rather than rushing through the visit.

03

We diagnose & explain

Once the concern is assessed, we explain what may be happening, what the diagnosis means and why it is happening.

04

We plan & follow up

You are given a treatment plan, practical instructions and a follow-up pathway so you know what happens next.

The philosophy
“You should leave the consultation knowing what is happening, why it is happening, and what the plan is.”

Medical care should leave room for questions. The consultation is where the diagnosis, treatment plan and follow-up are explained clearly.
